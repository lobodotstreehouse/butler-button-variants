01 — Identity

The mark & wordmark

Logo

The Butler Button mark is a silver-framed deep-violet tile carrying a white serving cloche, a peacock silhouette, and a white gloved hand — the butler serving experiences.

ButlerButton

Source vector: proposed/assets/logo.svg · 512×512 · corner radius 122 on the tile. Displayed with a 9px radius at nav size.

Logo colors

Tile / peacock#2B1B52Deep indigo-violet
Frame#C9C9D2Silver-gray border
Cloche / hand#FFFFFFWhite

Wordmark

Set as ButlerButton — "Butler" in the foreground text color, "Button" in the indigo accent. Full attribution reads Butler Button by VELTM.

Clear space & usage

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the cloche knob. Do not recolor the tile, stretch the mark, or place the light wordmark on low-contrast backgrounds.

Legacy live-header mark. The current public site header still carries the earlier gradient logo — a purple-to-gold sweep with a magenta accent. Those colors are documented on the palette page under Legacy accents so the transition to the violet mark above is intentional, not a mismatch.
